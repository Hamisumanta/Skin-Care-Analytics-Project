Project Brief - Sales Performance & Customer Analytics 

Date: 10th August 2026 

BACKGROUND 

The company has seen consistent revenue growth over the past two years across three channels - direct website (Shopify), Amazon, and 12 US retail partners. However, revenue growth has not translated clearly into visible margin improvement, and the business currently lacks a structured view of which products, regions, customers, and channels are driving profitable growth versus simply generating volume. With an investor update scheduled for early September 2026, the goal of this project is to turn two years of transaction data into clear, defensible business intelligence that Steve can walk into that meeting with confidently.

BUSINESS QUESTIONS 

This project will answer the following questions:

Which products and product lines drive the most revenue and gross margin?

How are discounts affecting margin across different product lines - is the volume lift worth it?

Who are Bloom & Butter's highest value customer segments, and what do they look like?

Which sales channels (website, Amazon, retail) are most profitable after costs?

Which US regions are performing well vs underperforming relative to revenue?

Are there seasonal or monthly demand patterns that should inform future planning?

DATA REQUIRED 

Shopify transaction export (.csv) all orders July 2024 – July 2026 including customer ID, product, channel, revenue, discounts

Product catalogue (.csv or .xlsx) SKU list with product names, lines, and cost of goods (COGS) Sarah (ops manager) 

Important note on data: Upon receipt I will cross-reference both files before beginning analysis to check for any gaps or inconsistencies. If I find anything that could affect the accuracy of the results I will flag it to you before proceeding - I won't build on uncertain numbers without telling you first. 

KNOWN DATA LIMITATIONS 

I want to be upfront about two limitations we discussed on the call so there are no surprises later: 

Retail partner sell-through: Your website data records wholesale orders to your retail partners, not individual end-consumer sales through those stores. This means retail performance in this analysis reflects orders to partners, not sales through them. This is worth noting in any investor conversation. 

Customer acquisition cost: Your transaction data alone won't give us a precise cost per acquisition figure since ad spend data lives separately. We can approximate customer value and behaviour through segmentation, but a full CAC calculation would require a separate data pull from your ad platforms - we can scope that as a follow-on piece if useful.

DELIVERABLES 

DELIVERABLE 1 

Interactive Power BI Dashboard A published dashboard covering: Revenue and gross margin by product, product line, channel, and region Discount impact analysis showing margin before and after discounts by product line Month-on-month and year-on-year revenue trends, Regional performance map across the US Customer segmentation breakdown (see Deliverable 2) The dashboard will be published to Power BI Service so you can access it from your browser without needing any software installed. 

DELIVERABLE 2 

Customer Segmentation: Customers segmented by value using Recency, Frequency, and Monetary (RFM) analysis - in plain terms, grouping customers by how recently they bought, how often they buy, and how much they spend. Segments will be clearly labelled in language that makes sense for your business (e.g. Champions, Loyal Customers, At Risk, and Lost) with a breakdown of how many customers sit in each group and what they're worth to the business. 

DELIVERABLE 3 

Written Recommendations Report: A short written report (not just charts) translating the findings into clear business recommendations - where to focus product investment, which customer segments to prioritize for retention, where discounting is working and where it isn't. Written in plain language, suitable for sharing directly with investors.
