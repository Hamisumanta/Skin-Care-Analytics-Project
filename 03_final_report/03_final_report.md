The dashboard covers the visuals. This document covers the so what.

EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

This business is a healthier business than it might feel from the inside. Revenue has grown consistently from last year to July, 2026 years by 132% and a core group of champion customers is genuinely carrying significant weight. Your instinct on this was right. Two things are quietly working against you:
Your discount strategy on daily defense 11.4% discount is destroying margin without proportional benefit by yielding 50.12% gross margin. This shows that higher discount rates are associated with lower gross margins.
Hydrating B5 serum is generating high profit but is not getting the investment attention.
None of these are crisis level problems. They are all fixable, and you will find exactly what I am saying when you look at the data.

SECTION 1: REVENUE BY PRODUCT 

What data shows Retinol Night Serum generate more profits and revenue that all other products. When an investor asks "where does your profit come from?" The answer is Retinol Night Serum. That is a clean, confident answer. 

RECOMMENDATION 1

Make Retinol Night Serum your hero story. In marketing, in product development and in investor conversations, lead with your Retinol Night Serum. The data backs it.

SECTION 2: CHANNEL PERFORMANCE 

Your direct website is your best channel - not just on volume but on margins. Customers who buy directly from you are with more per order than those who come from Amazon.
Amazon is doing what Amazon does, it gives you reach but takes its cut. The volume is real but the margin compression is also real and your instincts about this were correct. Retail appears to be the weakest profitability relative to its revenue. 

RECOMMENDATION 2

Invest in growing the direct channel, not Amazon. If your marketing budget has a choice between driving traffic to your site versus building an Amazon presence, your site wins on margin every time. Email marketing and loyalty mechanics on your own site will return more than Amazon and spend.

RECOMMENDATION 3

Consider an Instagram shop push for Retinol Night Serum specifically. Instagram shop has a small but healthy margin profile and RNS is your strongest product - this is a natural fit for the platform's visual format.

SECTION 3: DISCOUNT IMPACT ANALYSIS 

Higher discount rates are associated with lower gross margin, this suggests that discounting may be hurting the profit. If you look at the data, daily defense SFP 30 has 11.4% discount and 50.12% margin while Vitamin C serum has 1.6% discount and 69.65% margin. However a low margin on Holiday glow Gift Set with no discount indicates that there is an issue on pricing.

RECOMMENDATION 4

Set a discount ceiling policy before your next calendar. No product line should carry a discount greater than 10% unless you have data showing the volume lift covers the margin loss. Consider price adjustment on Holiday glow gift sets although you sell it seasonally. 

SECTION 4: CUSTOMER SEGMENTATION 

Your champion (15.04%) of your customers generates 67% of your revenue. Your instinct was right and the gap is larger than you probably expected.
The segment that needs urgent attention is can't lose them- 41.93% of customers who were high frequency buyers but have gone quietly recently. These people know everything, your brand, they spend and now they stopped. That is a retention failure worth addressing now.

RECOMMENDATION 5

Build a retention strategy around the champion before the investor meeting. Even having a plan- not a result yet, just a plan. Early access to the new SPF launch is a natural loyalty mechanic for this group.

 RECOMMENDATION 6

Launch a targeted win-back campaign for the can't lose them segment within the next 30 days. This group already trusts you. A personalized email- not generic promo blast- referencing what they previously bought and offering something relevant has a meaningful chance of reactivating them. This is the highest ROI retention action available to you right now.

RECOMMENDATION 7

Stop treating all new customers the same. 67% of your customers only ever buy once. That is an acquisition retention imbalance. A structured post purchase sequence - a check in email at day 7, a product pairing suggestion at day 21, a gentle incentive at day 45- cost almost nothing and could materially shift your repeat purchase rate over 12 months .

SECTION 5: REGIONAL PERFORMANCE 

What the data shows, the South and West are your strongest regions on every metric- revenue, order value, margin, gross profit, the Northeast sits at the bottom on all the others.

RECOMMENDATION 8

For the investor conversation, lead with south and west as your proven markets. These are the regions where your brand resonates most clearly and where margins is healthiest. A focused growth strategy in proven markets is a more compelling investor story than trying to fix underperforming ones.

SECTION 6: SEASONAL PATTERNS 

Q (November-December) is consistently your highest revenue period across both years. Likely driven by gifting and holiday shopping.
February - March show a sharp drop a pattern that repeats in both 2025 and 2026. Your current promotional activity does not appear to be structured around these patterns.

RECOMMENDATION 9

Plan your 2027 promotion calendar around these patterns, not against them. Save your deepest discount for January when customers are already in buying mode from habit but spending less- rather than running plat promotion all year. Use Q4 to run a price gift set and bundles.

SUMMARY OF RECOMMENDATIONS 

1. Lead with Retinol Night Serum in marketing and investor conversation.

2. Review discount strategy - test 10% ceiling high before.

3. Invest in growing direct website channel over Amazon.

4. Instagram shop push for Retinol Night Serum.

5. Set discount ceiling across all products.

6. Build loyalty programme for champions.

7. Post purchase email sequence for new customers.

8. Focus growth investment on South and west.

9. Restructure promotional calendar around seasonal patterns.
