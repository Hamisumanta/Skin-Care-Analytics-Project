# Skin Care-Analytics Project 

This project is a Power BI dashboard developed to analyze sales performance, customer retention, and regional trends for Bloom & Butter- a growing skincare brand. The main purpose of the dashboard is not simply to display sales numbers, but to turn transactional data into information that support better business decisions. 

The analysis uses transactional sales data as the foundation of the dashboard. From this data, key performance indicators are calculated using DAX (Data Analysis Expressions) in Power BI. Core measures include Total Revenue, Year-over-Year (YoY) Growth, and Average Order Value (AOV). These measures provide a high level view of the financial health of the business. Together, these metrics provide a starting point for understanding overall sales performance.

One of the most important parts of this project is the customer retention analysis. Instead of treating every customer in the same way, customers are segmented using RFM analysis, which stands for Recency, Frequency, and Monetary value. Recency looks at how recently a customer made a purchase, Frequency considers how often the customer purchases, and Monetary value measures how much the customer has spent. Combining these three dimensions makes it possible to identify different types of customers and understand their relationship with the brand.

The RFM model categorizes customers into meaningful groups such as Champions, Loyal Customers, Potential Loyalists, New Customers, At Risk, Can't Lose Them, Needs Attention, and Lost customers. For example, Champions represent highly engaged customers with strong purchasing behavior, while At Risk and Can't Lose Them segments can highlight customers whose engagement may be declining. 

The dashboard also examines regional performance across the business. Regions such as North, South, Central, and Northeast are presented using a Matrix table. 

Overall, the dashboard demonstrates how Power BI, DAX, customer segmentation, and business analysis can be combined to transform raw transactional data into practical insights. The project focuses on answering important business questions: 

How is revenue performing? 

Which customers are most valuable? 

Which customers may require retention 
efforts? 

How is performance distributed across regions? 

And where should the business focus its attention?

The goal is to provide a clear, interactive, and business-focused view of Bloom & Butter's performance while demonstrating an end-to-end approach to data analysis—from transactional data through KPI development, customer segmentation, visualization, and actionable business insight.
